# Sessions and news (times in UTC)

US and UK daylight saving shift most times by one hour. US data is fixed at **08:30 New York
time**: 12:30 UTC in summer (US DST), 13:30 UTC in winter. The US and UK switch on different
dates in March and Oct/Nov, so check both during those weeks.

## Sessions

| Window (UTC, approx.) | What gold usually does |
|---|---|
| 22:00-23:00 | Daily open after rollover. Wide spreads, thin. Don't trade. |
| 23:00-07:00 Asia | Builds a range. Chinese/Indian physical demand, SGE premium, PBoC news. Range often 30-50% of the daily ATR. |
| 07:00-08:00 London open | Frequently sweeps one side of the Asian range, then picks a direction. |
| 10:30 London time | LBMA Gold Price AM auction. Can pin price briefly. |
| 12:30/13:30 | US data. The biggest single-minute moves of the day. |
| 13:20-18:30 | COMEX gold most active (08:20-13:30 New York). Highest volume of the day. |
| 15:00 London time | LBMA PM auction, London starts closing. Reversals of the NY move are common after it. |
| 18:00-21:00 | Late NY. Liquidity fades; trend days often drift, reversal days often fade. |
| 21:00-22:00 | Rollover / maintenance break. Spreads very wide. Close or keep stops wide. |

Friday afternoon and the hours before a long weekend: flows square up, and weekend gap risk
matters if geopolitics is in play. Monday's open can gap $10-$50+.

## High-impact events

| Event | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FOMC decision | 8 per year, 14:00 NY, press conference 14:30 NY | Two moves: statement, then presser. Dot plot in Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec. |
| CPI | Monthly, ~10th-15th, 08:30 NY | Core CPI surprise moves yields -> gold. |
| Nonfarm payrolls | Usually first Friday, 08:30 NY | Wild first minute, often reverses within 15-30 min. |
| PCE | Monthly, late month, 08:30 NY | The Fed's preferred inflation gauge. |
| Retail sales, PPI, GDP, jobless claims | 08:30 NY | Medium impact, larger when the market is focused on growth. |
| ISM PMIs | 10:00 NY | Medium. |
| Fed chair / governor speeches | Varies | Can matter more than data in a policy-shift period. |
| Treasury auctions (10y, 30y) | ~13:00 NY | Weak auction -> yields up -> gold pressure. |
| Geopolitical headlines | Any time | Spikes often fade unless the event escalates. |

Veteran rules around news:

- Flat or reduced size 15 minutes before tier-1 data; no new entries in the first 1-5 minutes after.
- Expect stops to slip during the release. Size for the slippage, not just the stop.
- Trade the **reaction** to the number (does price hold the move after 15 minutes?), not the number itself.
- Check the calendar every day before the session; don't assume a quiet day.
