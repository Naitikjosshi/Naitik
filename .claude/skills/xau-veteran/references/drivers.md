# What moves gold

Ranked roughly by how often they matter day to day. Relationships shift over time, so confirm
them with recent price action rather than assuming them.

## 1. US real yields and Fed expectations
Gold pays no yield, so higher real yields (10y TIPS) raise the cost of holding it. Falling
yields and rising rate-cut odds are the classic tailwind. This link was very strong 2006-2021,
then weakened from 2022 as central bank buying kept gold rising with high real yields.
Still watch it: a sharp yield move on data day usually moves gold the opposite way within minutes.

## 2. The US dollar (DXY)
Gold is priced in dollars; a weaker dollar usually means higher gold. Watch EURUSD and USDJPY
as fast proxies. Gold rising *with* the dollar is a sign of strong safe-haven or central
bank demand.

## 3. Central bank and official buying
Since 2022, central banks (PBoC, NBP, CBRT, RBI and others) have been buying at record pace,
partly to diversify reserves away from dollars after sanctions risk became clear. This
creates a price-insensitive bid under dips. Monthly World Gold Council and PBoC reserve
data show the trend.

## 4. Safe-haven and geopolitics
Wars, sanctions, banking stress, government shutdowns, tariff shocks. Headline spikes often
fade within a day unless the situation escalates or changes policy. "Buy the rumour, sell the
news" around events everyone expects is common.

## 5. Investment flows
Gold ETF holdings (GLD, IAU and European/Asian ETFs), COMEX futures positioning (CFTC
Commitments of Traders, managed money net long). Crowded long positioning plus a hawkish
surprise is the setup for sharp $50-$150 flushes.

## 6. Physical demand
China and India jewellery and bar demand, the Shanghai premium/discount vs London,
Indian import duty changes, Diwali and wedding season, Chinese New Year. More important
for the medium-term floor than for intraday trading.

## 7. Cross-asset signals
- Silver (XAGUSD) and miners (GDX) confirming or diverging from gold.
- Equities: sharp risk-off can briefly drag gold down too (margin-call selling) before it rallies.
- Oil and inflation expectations (breakevens).

## Veteran habits
- Write down the macro bias **before** looking at the 5-minute chart.
- When the news is good for gold and price falls anyway, respect the price.
- Big round numbers ($100 handles) attract price, stops and options; expect noise there.
- After a parabolic month, size down: volatility rises in both directions.
